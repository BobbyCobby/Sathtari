# Basic Description

This description is the state of things when the player encounters the Sathtari.

## Physical Information

The Sathtari are a race of quadruped animals whose skin cells perform photosynthesis. They average four feet tall, and can stand on their rear legs for dexterity but almost always move around on all fours. They are incapable of absorbing energy outside of photosynthesis, nor storing it for more than a few days.

## Location

The Sathtari are based in the Athti system; their homeworld is Flaure. When the player encounters them, they do not have hyperdrive capabilities, but they develop them as the story progresses. Sathtari space is located west of the Deep, northwest of the Dirt Belt, and northeast of the northern tip of Coalition space.

## Current Social Structure

There are two main surviving races of Sathtari, the Tari Rithta and the Tari Shuda. These names mean literally "High Ones" and "Low Ones". The Tari Rithta have darker skin, and thus can absorb energy from their sun during overcast weather; they overheat in direct sunlight. The Tari Shuda, on the other hand, have lighter mottled skin which results in lethargy in overcast climates, or in extreme cases death.

## Belief Systems and Psychology

### Tari Rithta

The Tari Rithta believe it is the destiny and responsibity of the Sathtari (and any *hypothetical* alien species) to achieve scientific knowledge whatever the cost. In fact, the name for the Sathtari (invented by the Tari Rithta, as with the rest of their language) means "the ones who learn", i.e., we who unlike all others (that we know about) fulfill the meaning of life and build substantial knowledge generation upon generation. They have therefore enslaved the Tari Shuda to mine Hu'akhebe for heavy metals, for the great project "TBD"[^1], an underground particle accelerator going around the circumference of Flaure.

## Home System

### Flaure

The homeworld of the Sathtari is Flaure. In antiquity, it had many different climates, but when the player finds the Sathtari, the Tari Rithta have terraformed it to be overcast all the time--very comfortable for the Tari Rithta, depressing and even dangerous for the Tari Shuda.

### Hu'akhebe

Much closer to the star Athti is Hu'akhebe, a rocky world full to bursting with precious metals.

# Timeline

- 2300: The planet Flaure is split among many different factions and climates, with the equivalent of early 20th century human technology. The Tari Rithta faction rise to power by conquering their neighbors. Their name means "The High Ones" in their own language, and they have the most absorbant skin of all the Sathtari, which leads them to believe they have a right to rule.
- 2320: The first rudimentary rocket motors are developed in many places independently around this time.
- 2350: The first crewed spaceflight.
- 2355: The Tari Rithta develop the ancestor of the Findirombi interceptor and begin orbital military demonstrations.
- 2380: The first major world war begins, which is mainly fought in space.
- 2400: The Tari Rithta have won major victories and are universally accepted as the winner. They sign peace treaties which give them the sole right to deploy a space-based military.
- 2410: The Tari Rithta orbital blockade makes spaceflight by any other faction suicidal.
- 2450: 15 of the 92 members of the oligarchal Tungembiri Rithta council start the largest ever attempt to overthrow the government in response to the development of nuclear weapons. They form the Tari Thrifodi, the "Peaceful Ones", which gains power via a military coup inside the Tari Rithta government. They attempt to live up to their name but are forced to fight the Tari Rithta for the next 200 years.
- 2455: The Tari Thrifodi infiltrate a major military base of the Tari Rithta (the Thlipambi Fen) and appear to be poised to start a full revolution.
- 2456: The first ever Sathtari nuclear missile destroys the Tari Thrifodi capital city, . The Tari Tingko (the "Numerous Ones") and the Tari Shuda (the "Low Ones") end their war with each other and join together to convince the Tari Rithta to sign an anti-nuclear treaty, pretending to have developed nuclear weapons themselves. They were in fact working on the problem but had made little progress.
- 2490: The Tari Thrifodi have established a respectable empire despite their losses at with the Tari Thrifodi to fight the Tari Rithta, whose empire spans 40% of the planet.
- 2500: The Tari Rithta unleash an enormous nuclear arsenal and begin a massive world war.
- 2600: The Tari Rithta have conquered all small factions of Sathtari. Their only remaining opponents are the Tari Thrifodi and the Tari Shuda. The former make repeated attempts at peace, while the latter only wish revenge.
- 2650: The Tari Rithta defeat the Tari Thrifodi and sign a tentative peace treaty with the Tari Shuda.
- 2700: The Tari Rithta launch a final assault on the Tari Shuda only to find that the Tari Shuda have finally invented nuclear weapons. After both sides have exhausted their supply, fierce fighting ensues.
- 2775: The Tari Rithta conquer the last remaining Tari Shuda city, Futhrifpot "Disorder"[^2].
